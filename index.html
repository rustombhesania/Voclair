Made for lecture halls, conference venues and live events.

The room gets between the speaker and the audience.

Echo

In lecture halls and big meeting rooms, the voice bounces off the walls and comes back.

Noise

At events and busy venues, chatter, air conditioning and crowd noise bury the voice.

Reverb

In large, hard rooms, every word rings on and blurs into the next.

Today the fix is better microphones, acoustic treatment of the room, or hours of expert adjustment. Voclair cleans the voice as it is spoken.

Questions and answers

Is my audio stored?

No. Voclair runs on your own device, and nothing is kept.

Do I need new equipment?

No. Voclair is software. It works with the microphones and speakers you already have.

Is live mode ready?

Live mode is in testing. The result can be heard on a recording today.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on the venue and how Voclair is used. Request a quote, and Voclair will reply by email.
