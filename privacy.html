2. Overview

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics, tracking or advertising. Fonts, icons and scripts are hosted on the website itself; nothing is loaded from third parties. Personal data is processed only when you visit the site (server logs), when you send the quote form, and, with your consent, to remember your display settings.

3. Visiting the website (server logs)

When you open the website, your browser automatically sends information to the server of our hosting provider, which stores it in log files: your IP address, the date and time of the request, the page or file requested, the amount of data transferred, the referring page, and your browser and operating system.

This is needed to deliver the website and to keep it secure and stable, for example to detect attacks. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR; our legitimate interest is the secure, working operation of the website. The log files are deleted after [7] days at the latest, unless a security incident requires keeping a specific entry longer.

Hosting provider: [name, address; for example a provider with servers in the EU]. We have concluded a data processing agreement with the provider under Art. 28 GDPR.

4. Quote and early-access form

If you send the form, we process the information you enter: your email address, what you need (live sound, early access or not sure), the products you choose for early access, whether you want to be notified, and your optional message.

We use this only to answer your request and, if you request it, to prepare an offer. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR (steps taken at your request before a possible contract). We delete the data when your request has been dealt with, at the latest [12] months after the last contact, unless statutory retention periods (for example for commercial or tax records) require longer storage.

If you tick "Email me when it is available", we email you once the product you chose is available. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time by replying to any of these emails; we then delete your address from this list.

The form is delivered via [service, e.g. a form service or the hosting provider], which processes the data on our behalf under a data processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR). We never share your address with anyone else.

5. The demo: uploads and recordings

In the demo you can upload a recording or record 10 seconds with your microphone. In the current version, this audio is processed only in your browser on your own device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and it is not stored; it is gone when you close or reload the page.

The browser asks for permission before using your microphone, and only when you click "Record 10 seconds". You can refuse or withdraw this permission at any time in your browser settings.

The transcript preview is not active. If processing on a server is added in future, this policy will be updated before that happens.

6. Cookies and browser storage

This site sets no cookies. Only with your consent (the "Accept" button in the cookie notice) does your browser remember two settings in its local storage: your colour theme (light or dark) and your language (English or German). They stay on your device and are never sent to us. The legal basis is § 25 (1) TDDDG and Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR.

Your decision itself is stored on your device so that the question is not asked again on every visit. This is technically necessary (§ 25 (2) no. 2 TDDDG).

You can change or withdraw your consent at any time under "Cookie settings" at the bottom of every page. Withdrawing deletes the stored settings.

8. Your rights

You have the right to access your data (Art. 15 GDPR), to have it corrected (Art. 16), deleted (Art. 17) or its processing restricted (Art. 18), and to receive it in a portable format (Art. 20). Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7 (3)).

Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR): where we process data on the basis of legitimate interest (Art. 6 (1) (f)), you can object at any time for reasons arising from your particular situation.

To use these rights, contact us through the quote form on this website or by replying to any email from us.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection supervisory authority. The authority responsible for us is: Der Landesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz und die Informationsfreiheit Baden-Württemberg, Heilbronner Straße 35, 70191 Stuttgart, Germany, www.baden-wuerttemberg.datenschutz.de.

9. Other points

You do not have to provide any personal data to use this website. Without an email address we cannot answer a quote request. We do not use automated decision-making or profiling (Art. 22 GDPR). The website is delivered over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).
